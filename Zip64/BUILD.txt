ZIP64 library

Zip64File and EntryInputStream derive from Enter AG's ZIP64 implementation.
Original copyright and author notices are retained in the source files.
Unchanged helper classes are merged from SiardGui/lib/zip64.jar.

The writer buffers appends, spools large central directories and reuses
precompressed XML while preserving ZIP entry paths, CRCs and archive structure.

From SiardGui with JDK 17:
  .\gradlew.bat :Zip64:test :Zip64:jar

Output: Zip64/build/libs/zip64.jar. The application uses the Gradle project
dependency directly; no manual JAR replacement is necessary.
